## Notes

Killed a random worker container with SIGKILL every 25 s during the run (3 kills). Docker's restart policy restarted each one (like a Kubernetes pod restart). Duplicates are expected: a delivery whose HTTP request was in flight when its worker died is sent again after the reaper (2 min) re-queues it.
