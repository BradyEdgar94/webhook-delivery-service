## Notes

Changed the worker count mid-run with docker compose --scale: 4 -> 8 at ~15 s, then 8 -> 2 at ~40 s. Scale-down sends SIGTERM; workers drain in-flight deliveries and commit offsets before exiting, and Kafka rebalances their partitions (cooperative-sticky) to the survivors.
